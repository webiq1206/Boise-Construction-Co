## Implementation

viewport budget and retains `100svh` fallback. Header/bar observers already
publish measured heights excluding their safe-area padding. Each inset is counted
once. Construction's bottom bar is visible below1180px on the initial homepage;
hidden bars occupy zero budget. At desktop width the bar is absent.

The previous900px cap exposed104px of subsequent content at1920x1080. The new
Construction selector outranks that shared rule without editing canonical files.
`min-height` allows legitimate content to grow naturally; there is no clipping,
internal scroll box or blank spacer. The320x568 headline/primary-CTA exception and
short/enlarged-text conditions retain natural page scrolling.
