# House bible: Boise Construction Co

Brand: Boise Construction Co  
City placement: Eagle (foothills north of Eagle)  
Style: mountain-modern custom home, million-dollar-plus

## Architecture

- About 4,800 sq ft, low-slung shed and gable roofs
- Dark bronze standing-seam metal roof, deep overhangs, warm cedar soffits
- Smooth charcoal stucco with dry-stacked grey ledgestone masses at entry and garage
- Floor-to-ceiling black steel-framed windows
- Cantilevered covered entry; walnut pivot front door
- Flush-panel walnut-tone three-car side-load garage
- Board-formed concrete planters
- No Craftsman details (no tapered columns, exposed rafter tails, shake gables)

## Site

- One-acre foothills lot north of Eagle
- Native grasses, granite boulders, quaking aspens
- Dry sage and rabbitbrush slope behind; Boise Front in the distance
- Long broom-finished concrete drive
- Late afternoon high-desert light, wide clear sky

## Interior spine

- Wide-plank white oak floor through the main level
- White plaster walls; black steel windows from inside
- Floor-to-ceiling ledgestone fireplace in the great room
- Kitchen: rift-cut white oak flat-slab cabinets, 12-foot honed black quartzite island, plaster range hood, matte black hardware
- Primary bath: large-format honed limestone, curbless wet room, floating rift-cut white oak double vanity, matte black fixtures
