# Quarantined historical material

These files were copied into the Construction repository from a Remodeling backlink program. They are preserved to avoid losing historical receipts or duplicate-contact evidence. They are not a verified Construction baseline, current opportunity approval, valid Construction citation packet, or supported executable workflow.

The active free-only runner does not read or execute this directory. Do not submit its disavow file, send its drafts, reuse its photos, or treat its authority/traffic figures as current measurements. Historical `sent`, `approved`, contact, and publication fields must be reconciled privately before any future supported dispatch is enabled. Do not copy private contact history into new public outputs.

The archived API, contact-discovery and database modules are reference only. They can incur costs or mutate a database if executed directly. They are deliberately disconnected from every supported backlink command.
