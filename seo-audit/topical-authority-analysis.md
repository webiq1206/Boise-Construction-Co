## Strongest topics

Cost, contractor-selection, and process hubs are deep and well-linked. These are the site's authority anchors and should remain the primary internal-link destinations (cost guide currently over-linked — see internal-link-map.md).

## Weakest / thinnest

1. **ROI hub (6 clusters)** — thinnest. Add 2–3 comparison/definition posts (e.g. "kitchen vs bathroom ROI," "which remodels add the most value in Boise," "cost-to-value by Treasure Valley city").
2. **Outdoor-living** — adequate count but weakest link equity; needs internal-link boosting, not necessarily more posts.
3. **ADU subtopic** — not its own hub; ADU content is thin across the site despite being a dedicated *service*. Strong expansion candidate (see content-gap-analysis.md).
