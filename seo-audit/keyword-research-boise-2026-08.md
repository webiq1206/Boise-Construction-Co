# Local keyword research — Boise + Treasure Valley custom home building

Source: Ahrefs Keywords Explorer, country US, pulled 2026-08. Volumes are
monthly US estimates; difficulty (KD) is 0–100.

## Primary terms (target on the homepage + custom-home-builder pages)

| Keyword | Vol | KD | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| treasure valley home builders | 200 | 23 | Best volume/difficulty. Regional term. |
| custom home builder boise | 150 | low | Primary buy-intent term. Was missing from the homepage title — now added. |
| custom home builders boise idaho | 40 | 35 | Same intent, "idaho" modifier. |
| home builders boise / boise home builders | 150 / 100 | ~33 | Broad. Eyebrow "Boise Home Builder · Treasure Valley" targets it. |
| home builders in boise / boise idaho home builders | 100 | — | Phrase variants; covered by service + area copy. |
| new home construction boise | 50 | **4** | Easy win. Woven into homepage description. |
| barndominium builders idaho | 30 | **0** | Easy win. Owned by /services/shop-homes-barndominiums ("Idaho Shop Homes & Barndominiums"). |

## City terms (target the /areas/{city} and /services/{service}/{city} pages)

| Keyword | Vol | KD |
|---|---|---|
| home builders nampa idaho | 100 | 40 |
| home builders meridian idaho | 90 | 37 |
| home builders boise idaho | 70 | 58 (hardest) |
| custom home builder eagle idaho | 20 | — |
| home builders kuna idaho | 0 | — |

City pages render `Home Builder in {City}, Idaho`; city-service pages render
`Custom Home Building in {City}, Idaho`. Both capture the "{city} home builder"
intent. Kuna/Star/Middleton show ~0 local volume — keep the pages for coverage
and long-tail, do not over-invest.

## Modifiers people actually use (weave into body copy, not titles)

custom · new · local · affordable · luxury — e.g. "affordable home builders
boise idaho" (80), "local home builders boise id" (50), "new home builders boise
idaho" (60).

## Changes made from this research (2026-08)

- Homepage `<title>`: `Custom Home Builder in Boise, Idaho | Boise Construction
  Co` — leads with the primary buy-intent term, which the old brand-led title
  ("… | Treasure Valley Home Builder") missed. "Treasure Valley" still carried by
  the description, service title, and every location page.
- Homepage meta description: rewritten to include "custom home builder", "new
  home construction", and the city list.
- Homepage H1 and subhead: rewritten to drop the near-verbatim echo of the old
  remodeling tagline ("… with clarity and confidence") and lead with the keyword.
